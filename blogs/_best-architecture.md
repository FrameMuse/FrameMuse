Apparently, big corporations have an awkward architecture or no architecture at all pretty much often.

Experience made me understand that big something != better something. They just do things for money, that’s it.

As a developer in any company, you will always do what they need at they moment to get more money, not what the project has to do, e.g. refactoring - because refactoring has no value for a client, you have to justify all the time.

The best architecture you can have in your life is the one you built yourself in your own projects without deadlines.
