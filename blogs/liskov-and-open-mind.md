# Liskov & Open Mind

Only now I realized what it actually means despite of many read articles.

Now I understand it as a practical aspect rather theoretical.

It might seem not as important, though by these tiny things I'm getting even better as developer.

E.G. Now I define `Blob` for files, not actual `File`, which seems more reasonable.

This is because often I don't need name of a file or other properties and by putting more wider type I'm making it more accessible and more likely to be reused.

Basically Liskov's substitution rule says that you should define the least needed requirement for your system, there are many reasons for that, you can see the internet.

My understanding how I can apply this to my code and Why I need it has come only recently when hit the threshold when I eagerly study so much, it gets difficult to know what you don't know and how it can be applied. Especially when the wording is very abstract without clear examples, there comes a feeling of powerless.

Now I'm trying to be open to things I don't understand in order to actually come to a Real understanding, instead of skipping things it's hard to understand or considering them as “not a use case For Me”. Now I'm trying to see how I could apply them and why they became good practices in the first place.

And I'm not stopping here, in a race of trying to broaden my openness even further I'm investigating how to apply knowledge from other areas of life to the programming. Like the music theory, which first was open by Pythagoras 🤯, I thought he was only about Trigonometry.

If you don’t fully understand something, try to play/study/apply it in your very specific case, when you come back to the abstraction, you will open it again.

You go from abstraction to specificity and back again to open new things and applicable areas if shortly.

It gives me insights that everything is connected to everything.
